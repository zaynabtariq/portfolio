### Project Case Study Format

Each major project should answer:

PROBLEM
What needed to change?

CONTEXT
Why did it matter?

CONSTRAINTS
What made it hard?

DECISION
What did Zaynab choose?

BUILD
What was implemented?

TRADEOFFS
Why this solution?

RESULT
What happened?

TECHNICAL DETAILS
Only the details that demonstrate depth.

Avoid résumé bullets as the primary storytelling format.
